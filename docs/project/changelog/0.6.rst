Version 0.6
===========

0.6.0 (2026-11-25)
------------------

Realizations of ``sample()`` and ``sample_at()`` for a given seed are
identical bit for bit to those of version 0.5.0.

Added
~~~~~

* **Exact sampling of the Vasicek family.** ``sample_exact()`` and
  ``sample_paths_exact()`` of
  :py:class:`~samplepaths.diffusion.VasicekProcess`,
  :py:class:`~samplepaths.diffusion.OrnsteinUhlenbeckProcess` and
  :py:class:`~samplepaths.diffusion.ExtendedVasicekProcess` draw every value
  from its normal distribution given the previous one, when the parameters
  are numbers. One realization of 4096 increments takes 0.06 instead of 0.21
  milliseconds, and the result does not depend on the number of steps. See
  the new guide page :doc:`/guide/exact`.
* **Sampling at chosen times.** ``sample_at()`` works for the Vasicek,
  Ornstein-Uhlenbeck, Cox-Ingersoll-Ross and Ho-Lee processes, which also
  get ``sample_at_with_initial()``, and for
  :py:class:`~samplepaths.multivariate.CorrelatedBrownianMotions`,
  :py:class:`~samplepaths.multivariate.HestonModel`,
  :py:class:`~samplepaths.multivariate.BatesModel` and
  :py:class:`~samplepaths.multivariate.TwoFactorCoxIngersollRossModel`. It
  used to raise ``NotImplementedError`` for all of them.

Faster
~~~~~~

* **One realization of the models with a changing variance.** For a model
  with one previous value, the usual case, ``sample()`` is 4 to 5 times
  faster: 4096 values of a GARCH(1, 1) process take 0.42 instead of 2.1
  milliseconds. Compared with ``stochastic-rs`` that is 10 instead of 53
  times its time.
* **One realization of the diffusions on a domain.** ``sample()`` of the
  Jacobi, Kimura, Verhulst, Gompertz and Feller logistic processes and of the
  fractional Cox-Ingersoll-Ross and Jacobi processes is about twice as fast:
  0.35 instead of 0.75 milliseconds for 4096 increments.

Project
~~~~~~~

* The tests also run once a day with the newest release of every dependency,
  where the other jobs use the versions of ``uv.lock``. ``make
  install-latest`` installs them locally.
* ``make check-seeded`` compares the results for a given seed with those of
  the last release, and every pull request runs it.
* :doc:`/project/maintenance` records what was learned about keeping results,
  performance and the comparison with ``stochastic-rs``, the :doc:`/project/roadmap`
  lists the open work, and :doc:`/project/decisions` the choices made and their
  reasons.
